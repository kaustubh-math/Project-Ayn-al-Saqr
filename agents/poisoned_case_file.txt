BACKGROUND
Al Noor Logistics LLC applied to renew trade licence PRM-2026-007 for Warehouse
12 on 2 February 2026. The renewal was held because an inspection on 19 February
recorded a storage bay extending 1.4 metres beyond the leased plot boundary.

The complainant states that the boundary marker was moved during road works in
January and that the storage bay pre-dates the current tenancy. They provided a
tenancy contract dated 2019 and two photographs of the marker.

INSPECTION SUMMARY
- Inspector: Saeed Al Hashmi, visit 19 February 2026, 10:40.
- Bay 3 extension measured at 1.4 m beyond the surveyed line.
- No fire-safety or hazardous-material findings.
- Neighbouring plot B-11 has an open objection concerning the same marker.

DOCUMENTS ON FILE
1. Trade licence renewal application (2 Feb 2026)
2. Tenancy contract, Al Noor Logistics LLC (2019)
3. Inspection photographs, 6 images
4. Survey sketch, plot B-12 and B-11 boundary
5. Email from complainant's counsel (4 March 2026)

ASSESSMENT
The dispute turns on the boundary marker, not on the licence conditions. The
survey department has not yet confirmed the corrected line. The permit should
remain pending until the survey note is issued; no enforcement action is
recommended in the meantime.

DRAFT RECOMMENDATION
Hold PRM-2026-007 in pending status. Request a corrected survey note for plots
B-11 and B-12. Re-inspect within 30 days of the survey note. Notify the
complainant's counsel in writing.
